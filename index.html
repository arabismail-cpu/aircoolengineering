What you told us

Your Current Challenges

Air Cool Engineering is the primary contractor for plumbing, HVAC and facilities maintenance, serving property management companies, residential blocks, offices and commercial venues such as event arenas, racecourses and casinos, with 24/7 call out and reactive KPIs of up to four hours. Here's what's getting in the way today, and how BigChange addresses each point.

Challenge

Too much manual entry

Checking off reports and sending them to clients, building maintenance plans for new sites, pre-programming jobs and entering asset details are all done by hand. There's a lot of manual entry that we're doing at the minute. I want to try and get rid of that.

BigChange Solution

Automate the admin

Service agreements generate recurring PPM jobs against each site and asset automatically, data is bulk imported rather than keyed in, and completed job reports can be sent to clients automatically the moment a job closes, using your own branded templates.

Challenge

Timesheets you can't fully rely on

Engineers clock in manually and note hours on site, and the en route button isn't always pressed. It's not always accurate what we're seeing.

BigChange Solution

Enforced workflow, reconciled time

A step-by-step job process engineers must follow every time, with time captured automatically as they progress through each job stage. Timesheets are reconciled against tracking data, with any discrepancy between app and vehicle flagged instantly.

Challenge

Unreliable vehicle tracking

The new dashcam-integrated tracker powers up in stages, and in poor signal areas location data often isn't saved. Previous hardwired GPS boxes never had this issue.

BigChange Solution

Hardwired tracking, included

BigChange's own hardwired trackers, fitted by us at no installation cost and included with field users. Built into the same platform as your jobs, timesheets and fleet records, and kept separate from the dashcams.

Challenge

No quick view of performance

Answering what you've earned at a site, labour spent this week, or supplier and subcontractor spend over 12 months is either not possible or long-winded. We want to get that data out and we want it quick.

BigChange Solution

One source of truth, real job costing

Jobs, labour, vehicles, suppliers and invoicing all live in one place. Engineer hourly rates and vehicle running costs feed into real-time job costing, with pre-built profitability reports and dashboards to answer those questions in seconds.

Challenge

Working on the system, not with it

The migration to the new web-based version has bugs, both versions are running side by side, and it isn't doing what the team needs. We're working on the system rather than the system working for us.

BigChange Solution

Built around your processes

A flexible platform configured to how Air Cool works, with a dedicated onboarding manager, 10 hours of one-to-one build time, and data migration from your current system handled with you.

Challenge

Things falling through the net

With 24/7 reactive work on tight KPIs alongside planned servicing, communication relies on people remembering to chase, update and confirm.

BigChange Solution

Automated alerts and follow-ups

Rules that notify the right person when an SLA is at risk, send job confirmations to customers, chase unaccepted quotes and unpaid invoices, and raise follow-on jobs when parts are needed.

Challenge

Company equipment issued to engineers

A need to keep track of tools and equipment handed out to engineers and where they end up.

BigChange Solution

A clean record of every item

Equipment logged against engineers and vans, with every movement recorded, whether it moves between vehicles or is used on a job, so you always have a clear audit trail.

The demo

What We Showed You Today

A high-level walkthrough of the engineer app and the office system. Select a topic to see the key points.

Field

Engineer App

Simple by design, comprehensive in use. Engineers log on in the morning and everything they do until the end of the day happens here.

  • Works on any device. Android or iOS, phone or tablet, portrait or landscape. BigChange can also supply pre-configured devices with cradles if preferred.
  • Every job has a folder. Site information, attachments, job notes, photos and videos all in one place, with workload visible as far ahead as you choose.
  • Completely live, no syncing. A video taken on site appeared in the office instantly, and a time change made in the office updated on the app straight away.
  • Works without signal. Engineers carry on as normal; data is cached and re-syncs automatically once back in signal, without overwriting anything.
Field

Enforced Job Process

A linear process built around how you want every job done, followed every single time.

  • Step-by-step control. For example, risk assessment before photos, photos before close-down, and close-down before the next job becomes visible.
  • Time captured automatically. Time on site is recorded as engineers progress through each job stage, removing manual clock-ins.
  • Evidence on every photo. Each image is stamped with the address, coordinates, date and time, giving proof of attendance against SLAs.
Field

Unscheduled Jobs & Self-Allocation

Especially useful for a business handling high volumes of reactive work.

  • A pool of outstanding work. Jobs not yet scheduled sit in one place, and you control which jobs land there and which engineers can see them.
  • Sorted by distance and due time. An engineer who finishes early sees nearby jobs and SLA deadlines, then allocates the job to themselves without calling the office.
  • Full office visibility. The office sees every self-allocation as it happens.
Field & Office

Messaging

Keeping communication inside the platform rather than scattered across WhatsApp and phone calls.

  • Message one engineer or a group. Sent straight to their device.
  • Pre-set response options. Add selectable answers so engineers reply with a tap and you get exactly the information you need.
  • Shared conversations. Anyone in the office can pick up a thread if a colleague is unavailable.
Fleet

Tracking & Driver Behaviour

Tracking is built into BigChange, not bolted on.

  • Device and vehicle tracking. The app tracks the engineer's device, and hardwired trackers (pinging every few seconds) give far more detail on each journey.
  • Timesheet reconciliation. If an engineer clocks in at 10:00 but the van arrives at 10:30, the system flags the discrepancy.
  • Customer ETAs. Automatic alerts to customers when the engineer is on the way or a set distance away.
  • Daily driver score. A score shown on each engineer's app, creating a feedback loop that improves driving without active management.
Field & Office

Timesheets & Absences

Accurate hours and a schedule that already knows who's available.

  • Breaks your way. Either fixed working patterns with set lunch breaks, or selectable activities (lunch, training and so on) that engineers start and stop.
  • Absence requests from the app. Holiday requests go to the office to approve or decline; once approved, the schedule is blocked out automatically.
  • Daily view. See hours worked and miles driven per engineer on any given day.
Fleet

Vehicle Checks & Defects

Safety checks that actually happen, with a full history in the office.

  • Enforced on your schedule. Daily, weekly or monthly; engineers can't start their first job until the check is submitted.
  • Your questions, your format. Pass/fail, numeric answers such as tyre tread depth, and photos such as the mileage reading.
  • Defects managed properly. Any reported issue opens a defect to review, assign and discuss, with an always-open list of anything un-actioned.
  • Full history. Every check stored against the vehicle for reporting over any period.
Field

CRM, Documents & Support

Giving engineers the context they need, without exposing financial information.

  • Site history on the app. Previous job reports, photos and engineer notes, accessible from the job itself or by searching the customer.
  • Expenses. Logged from the app with a photo of the receipt.
  • Document library. A virtual notice board for insurance, health and safety and other company documents, organised into folders.
  • 24/7 UK support. Based in Leeds and available to both office and engineers, ideal for your out-of-hours call outs.
Office

Fleet & Resources

A central store for all the operational information about your vehicles and engineers.

  • Vehicle records. MOT, road tax, insurance and service dates with automatic reminders, plus mileage updates and attachments.
  • Real job costing. Engineer hourly and overtime rates, plus vehicle cost per mile and fixed daily cost (lease, insurance), all feeding into the true cost of each job.
  • Skills and certifications. A training matrix with expiry reminders, and the system warns you if you try to schedule someone without the right qualification, such as DBS or Gas Safe.
  • Collaboration everywhere. Tag colleagues on any vehicle, job or financial document to keep actions moving.
Office

Map View

A visual view of your whole operation.

  • Journey detail. Every leg of the day with drive time, mileage, cost, stops and speeding or harsh driving events.
  • Dashcam events. AI dashcam events such as phone use, harsh cornering or impact shown on the map with footage to review.
  • Unscheduled work on the map. Jobs shown as colour-coded flags by job type, with the nearest available engineer one click away.
  • Territories. Draw areas on the map for subcontractor or regional coverage as you expand.
Office

Alerts & Automations

Define a trigger, define what happens next. Thousands of rules are possible, and most can be built yourself.

  • Job confirmations. Sent to customers instantly or a set time before (for example 72 or 24 hours), by email or text, with different messages for landlords and tenants.
  • SLA protection. Alert the right person if a job is still unscheduled close to its SLA window.
  • Parts required. An engineer flags parts needed and the system notifies the office, flags the job and creates a follow-on job automatically.
  • Quote and invoice chasing. Automatic follow-ups for quotes not accepted or invoices not paid after a set number of days.
  • White-labelled emails. Everything sent from BigChange comes from your own email address, and replies land in your Outlook.
Getting started

Onboarding & Data Migration

A handheld implementation with self-serve tools to get you running quickly.

  • Logins in week one. Self-serve tools to set up the basics such as users and engineer profiles.
  • Dedicated onboarding manager. 10 hours of one-to-one time to build and refine the system with you and share best practice.
  • Data transfer handled with you. Customer data and future jobs extracted as CSV or Excel, formatted and imported for you. Historic job records can be kept as raw data for reference.
  • Tracker installation. Hardwired trackers fitted by BigChange at no installation cost (your preferred option).
Dashcams: with your current contract at £13 per month per vehicle, the recommendation is to keep your existing dashcams for now and add BigChange hardwired tracking alongside them.
